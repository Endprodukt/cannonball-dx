As of October 2026:

- ROAD ENHANCEMENT (VIDEO ENHANCEMENTS menu, 2X engine resolution and above) smooths the
  road edges and markings between native scanlines. The road data itself is still only
  accurate to one native pixel, so a slight waviness of about one native pixel remains
  visible along road edges on very large displays. Native scanlines are rendered exactly
  as before, so sprite and shadow alignment is unaffected. The option can be switched off.

As of September 2025:

- Audio may not play after system restart. This is caused by the SDL device ordering
  changing on some systems at each boot. Future revision may include the option to select
  audio device by name to resolve this.
  
- Some steering wheels expose more buttons or controls than SDL can use, and in these cases
  some buttons or controls may not be recognised. A mapping entry is required in
  res/gamecontrollerdb.txt for affected devices.
